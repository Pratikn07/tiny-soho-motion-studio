# Reel 01: "It's Not the Sugar" (worked example)

The first finished reel, made 5 Oct 2026, for posting around 29 Oct. 30.1 seconds, 8 scenes. Goal:
comments and debate. Use it as the model for shape, length and tone.

## The brief it came from
```
TITLE: It's Not the Sugar
POST BY: 29 Oct 2026
GOAL: comments
ANGLE: Halloween meltdowns are probably caused by the night itself, not the candy.
HOOK: That Halloween meltdown? It's probably NOT the sugar.
TWIST: In a study, moms who were told their kid had sugar rated them more hyper. The drink was sugar-free.
TAKEAWAY: Skip the candy guilt and protect the bedtime.
SAVE PROMPT: Save this for Halloween night.
FACTS:
- Sugar does not change children's behavior | Wolraich et al., JAMA, 1995 (meta-analysis of double-blind studies)
- Mothers told their son had sugar rated him more hyperactive; every drink was sugar-free | Hoover & Milich, 1994
SERIES: Halloween
EMOTION: playful, guilt lifted
TREATMENT: cut-paper collage, sticker cut-outs, a new paper colour per scene
SIGNATURE MOMENT: the SUGAR label peels off the cup to show SUGAR-FREE
```

## Why it works
- **Contrarian hook.** It contradicts what most parents believe, in the first line, and the cover frame
  already says it.
- **One big question.** "So… what IS it?" holds viewers through the middle.
- **Conflict → curiosity → twist.** Myth, then the study, then the sugar-free cup.
- **Every line has its own visual** ("Comprehension Maxxing"), so the reel works with the sound off.
- **Clock anchor.** The spinning clock carries "a bedtime two hours late" and becomes the moon.
- **Mirror close.** It opens on a grumpy Anaika in costume and ends on her asleep in the same costume.
- **Save prompt tied to a future moment.** "Save this for Halloween night" gives a reason to save now.
- **Kind to everyone.** It removes guilt instead of adding it, and blames nobody.

## Fact care
- The study subjects were 5–7-year-old boys, so the script says "kid", not "toddler".
- "Probably" in the hook, because one night's meltdown can have many causes.
