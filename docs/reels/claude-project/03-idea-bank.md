# Tiny Soho reels: idea bank

Ideas so far, what was rejected and why, and what the account's data says. As of 7 Oct 2026.

## What the data says
- Earlier Metricool numbers (126 carousels, 23 Jul – 3 Oct 2026): Halloween posts have the best share rate;
  sleep is the most-asked topic after food.
- The median reel viewer watches about 7 seconds, so the point must land in the first 3.
- The owner's storytelling library (816 saved reels, 5,441 analysed story beats, 2,302 named techniques)
  has no Halloween reels, so Halloween ideas need fresh thinking rather than copying a pattern.

## Techniques that work in this format
- **Contrarian hook**: say the opposite of what parents believe ("It's probably NOT the sugar").
- **One big question** planted early and answered late.
- **Conflict → curiosity → twist.**
- **Comprehension Maxxing**: every voice line has its own matching visual, so it works with sound off.
- **Countdown or clock anchor**: a clock, a timeline or a countdown that carries the story.
- **Mirror close**: the last shot echoes the first.
- **Save prompt tied to a future moment**: "Save this for Halloween night."
