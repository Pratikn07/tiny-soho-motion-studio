# Tiny Soho reels: brand and style

How every @tinysoho reel looks and sounds. Use it to judge whether an idea can be shown in this style.
Source: the reel project's style sheet, 7 Oct 2026.

## Brand
- One brand with Tiny Soho as its face. @tinysoho is the voice people meet on Instagram;
  mycuratedhaven.com is where the bio link sends them.
- Every reel ends on the Tiny Soho heart lockup with "More at mycuratedhaven.com · link in bio".
- Tone: calm, realistic motherhood. No shaming of kids, parents or food. No medical advice. Uncertain
  claims say "probably". US spelling.

## Cast (AI-generated)
- **Anaika**: a toddler, the main character. Expressions used so far: grumpy pout, curious, asleep.
- **Her mum.**
- Keep poses gentle. A pout works; a wailing, kicking toddler does not (image tools block it, and it
  reads as distress).

## Frame
- 9:16 (1080 × 1920), 20–30 seconds.
- The first frame is the cover: the hook line, the character and the lockup are all readable on it.
  Nothing that matters fades in.
- Instagram covers the bottom fifth and the right edge, so text sits in the upper and middle part of the
  frame.

## Rules that hold for every look
- One scene per sentence, about 2.5–4 seconds each.
- Scenes change through an object (a candy flies to the centre and becomes the next scene's hero), not a
  plain cut.
- One idea and one signature move per scene. Everything else stays quiet.
- Motion is calm and editorial: rises, slides, pops, stamps, a slow push-in. No wiggles, hops or bounces.
- Every reel's treatment is different; see `04-series-and-looks.md`. Reel 01's treatment was cut-paper
  collage: textured paper in a new colour per scene, and cut-out stickers with a warm-white edge and a
  soft shadow. That is one treatment, not the house style.

## Colour
| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Cocoa ink | `#321708` | Text on light paper |
| Cocoa card | `#2A150B` | Dark scene paper |
| Cream paper | `#F4EADC` | Light scene paper |
| Rose | `#B0544C` | The one emphasis word per line |
| Gold | `#F3C46B` | Subtitles on dark paper |
| Night | `#1F2340` | Night scenes |
| Sage | `#3F5A47` | Reveal scenes |
| Pumpkin | `#E8833A` | Illustrations only, never text |

## Type
- Headlines: Fraunces, sentence case, large (readable on a phone at a glance).
- One italic rose emphasis word per line at most, for example "It's not the *sugar.*"
- Subtitles: Fraunces italic, burned in word by word.
- Labels: Inter, spaced capitals, for tags like MASKS or 7:42 PM.

## What the style can show well
- A claim stamped MYTH or TRUE, a meter swinging, a label peeling off, a clock spinning, a list of items
  popping in on their words, a split screen, a loop diagram with a travelling dot, a crossed-out word.
- Objects and props as stickers (candy, masks, cups, clocks, books, toys).
- Anaika and her mum in a few simple poses.

## What it cannot show well
- Real footage, crowds, complex action, or many characters at once.
- Long lists, small text or dense charts.

## Sound
- A warm mom narrator (the owner's cloned voice) on top.
- A soft original music bed under the voice.
- One sound per on-screen action (a thump, a pop, a stamp, a ding).
