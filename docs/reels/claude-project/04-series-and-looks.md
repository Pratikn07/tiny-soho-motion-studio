# Tiny Soho reels: series and looks

Every reel has three layers. The brand never changes, a series shares a theme, and every reel gets a fresh
treatment. A viewer should recognise Tiny Soho in every reel, recognise the series across its reels, and
never feel they have seen this reel before.

## The three layers

| Layer | What it covers | How often it changes |
|---|---|---|
| **Brand** | Fraunces and Inter; the one rose emphasis word; the narrator's voice; Anaika and her mum; calm, kind tone; calm motion (no wiggle, hop or bounce); the heart lockup end card | Never |
| **Series theme** | The world and mood; one accent colour on top of the brand palette; 2–3 recurring motifs; the sound signature (instruments, a short musical motif) | Once per series |
| **Reel treatment** | The medium and texture; layout system; transition style; the signature moment; pacing | Every reel. Never repeat a treatment inside a series |

A series theme is not a fixed style. The Halloween series can be collage in one reel, a diary in the next
and paper theatre after that. What ties them together is the night, the pumpkin glow, the porch light and
the music-box motif.

## Series

### Halloween: "Halloween at Anaika's"
- **World and mood:** the evening of 31 Oct, dusk into night, cosy with a little spook. Never scary.
- **Accent colour:** pumpkin glow `#E8833A` (illustrations only) against night `#1F2340`.
- **Recurring motifs:** Anaika's pumpkin costume; the porch light and doorbell; the clock that becomes the
  moon.
- **Sound signature:** plucked strings and celesta, ending on a music-box lullaby.
- **Reels so far:**

| Reel | Treatment | Signature moment |
|---|---|---|
| 01 It's Not the Sugar | Cut-paper collage, sticker cut-outs, a new paper colour per scene | The SUGAR label peels off the cup to show SUGAR-FREE |

- **Treatments still open for this series:** a tired mum's diary (lined paper, two-colour riso print,
  handwritten crossings-out); paper theatre or shadow puppets on a lit screen; trading cards or yearbook
  portraits (good for "5 types of moms"); a porch-camera view (fixed frame, the night passing).

### Sleep: "After lights out"
- **World and mood:** bedtime and the night after it. Calm, honest, a bit wry.
- **Accent colour:** moonlight cream `#F4EADC` on night `#161A33`.
- **Recurring motifs:** the moon; the bedroom door with a strip of hall light; the clock.
- **Sound signature:** soft felt piano, slower pace.
- **Reels so far:** none.

### Myths: "Myth or not"
- **World and mood:** the evidence desk. Curious, fair, never smug.
- **Accent colour:** sage `#3F5A47` for the verdict.
- **Recurring motifs:** the MYTH / TRUE / IT DEPENDS stamp; a source line under every claim; a "parent's
  rating" style meter.
- **Sound signature:** a light pizzicato pulse and a firm stamp sound on the verdict.
- **Reels so far:** none. (Reel 01 is also a myth reel; it belongs to Halloween first.)

### Standalone
For a reel that fits no series. It still keeps the brand layer and gets a fresh treatment.

New series are added here when the owner agrees on one.

## Treatment menu
The full menu, with styles sorted for the brand, moves, transitions and the reference reels behind them, is the
motion library: `runner/motion-library.md`. The Studio Mac reads it on every look and storyboard. The short list
below is the original set; pick from or combine either. Each new treatment is new engine code, so one new treatment per reel is
the target; recombining earlier ones is cheaper.

| Treatment | Feels | Works for |
|---|---|---|
| Cut-paper collage (reel 01) | Playful, handmade | Myth-busting, lists |
| Tired mum's diary, riso print | Wry, honest | Rough weeks, schedules, confessions |
| Paper theatre / shadow puppets | Storybook, warm | Bedtime, toddler's-eye stories |
| Trading cards / yearbook portraits | Funny, shareable | "Types of moms", "which toddler is yours" |
| Evidence board with stamps and string | Curious, investigative | Myths, studies |
| Chalkboard or classroom poster | Clear, teacherly | How-tos, "what toddlers can do" |
| Embroidery or felt | Tender, slow | Emotional moments, grandparents |
| Fixed camera (porch, nursery door) | Observational, time passing | A night or a day in one frame |
| Film photo album, Polaroids | Nostalgic | Then-and-now, milestones |

## Where references come from
- **savee.com** moodboards for mood and emotion. The owner browses and attaches screenshots.
- **prompt-motion.com**: motion videos made with Claude and their prompts. Useful for techniques the
  engine can do in code.
- **motionin.design**: interactive web sections. Useful for single techniques (kinetic type, grain,
  depth), not for whole looks.
- Reference reels broken down in Studio.
