# Tiny Soho Studio

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Content creators and marketers turning finished image carousels into short videos. The supplied examples feature parenting moments and food recipes with embedded text.

## Product Purpose
Give an existing carousel a meaningful five-second story while preserving its typography, branding, layout and exact image aspect ratio. The creator reviews the suggested story and moving area before making a video.

## Capabilities and Constraints
WAN is the intended video engine. NVIDIA vision analysis is a preferred future integration, not a working feature of this UI milestone. Users may upload or paste images of different proportions; no fixed aspect ratio is imposed. Original text remains protected. Cinema Studio, Canvas and Director expansion are deferred.

This milestone is a local interactive UI. It uses three user-supplied examples and one existing WAN result, clearly marked as a sample with a known hair/caption artifact. Uploaded images are decoded in the browser and are not sent to a provider. Story suggestions for the examples are authored examples, not claimed AI analysis. Live generation is not connected here. The existing local app remains accessible at /legacy. Hosted production is outside this milestone.

## Brand Commitments
The user approved a light, premium editorial interface: ivory and white, black typography and controls, restrained serif headings, fine rules, generous space. The uploaded artwork leads the workspace. Controls use plain creator language.

## Evidence on Hand
Three supplied Tiny Soho slides (parenting, toddler meal prep, salmon cakes). A real five-second meal-prep video has a coherent eating action, preserved typography and a remaining hair/caption boundary defect. All UI samples are labelled; no completion status implies a fresh provider job.

## Product Principles
- Preserve the creator's finished artwork.
- Show a meaningful action with a beginning and reaction.
- Keep technical model choices out of the primary workflow.
- Review and correct the proposed moving area before generation.
- Distinguish demonstration content from an actual generated result.
